Breathalyzer Plus {version}
Mod manager and launcher for Savage Wind (Breath of the Wild on PC), with our own mods. Made by giruka.

HOW TO START

1. Unpack this whole zip: right-click it, choose Extract All.
   A good place: next to your Savage Wind folder, for example Documents\SavageWind\Breathalyzer.
   Not in Program Files.
2. Open "Start Breathalyzer".
3. The first time, show it your Savage Wind folder (the folder with savagewind.exe in it).
4. Pick your mods: each one has a title and a short description. Turn on the ones you want.
   You can change this later.
5. Press Play.

WHAT YOU NEED

- Savage Wind 0.4.1-alpha.4, with its game files set up from your own copy of the game (version 1.9.0).
- Nothing else. Breathalyzer brings its own Python. You don't install anything.

THE MODS

The mods in this zip are made by us, for Breathalyzer. Mods that change the game are off until you turn them on.
Some are built from your own game files the first time you turn them on.
The list: app\bundled-mods.json

IF WINDOWS OR YOUR ANTIVIRUS STOPS IT

- "Windows protected your PC": press "More info", then "Run anyway".
  Start Breathalyzer is a small new program that isn't signed yet.
- If your antivirus holds Start Breathalyzer back, open app\Start Breathalyzer (backup).cmd instead.

GOOD TO KNOW

- No game files are in this zip, and no mods made by other people.
- Breathalyzer never goes online by itself.
- Your settings and mods stay when you update: unpack the new version and use it instead.
- Help: app\docs\launcher.md
